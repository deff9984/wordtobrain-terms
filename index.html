10. Research and Eye-Tracking Participation

The App offers an optional, consent-gated research programme (reading-speed science). Participation is voluntary, is not required to use the App, and requires an Arena account and your explicit consent; nothing is recorded before you consent. If you participate, the data collected is limited to: profile answers given as ranges only ("bins" — age band, gender, education level, how many books you read, native language, and your goal; never exact values, and free text is never accepted) and behavioral events from a fixed whitelist (app open/close, reading sessions — words, seconds, average words per minute — pauses and resumes, onboarding completion, and arena results).

Within the research programme you may separately opt in to camera-based eye tracking (gaze) during the daily challenge; it requires its own consent. The camera runs only in the App, only during a consented session, and never in the background. Gaze processing happens on your device — camera frames never leave it and are never uploaded. Only numeric aggregates are uploaded once per completed session: per-word fixation times and counts, blinks, time off-screen, scaled pupil-size averages, a calibration-error estimate, capture quality, and an optional self-reported difficulty rating.

You can withdraw research consent or turn eye tracking off at any time in the App; recording stops immediately, and the data already stored is erased together with your account (Section 9).
